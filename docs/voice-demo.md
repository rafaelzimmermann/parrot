# Voice demo

`voice-demo.wav` is synthesized audio generated by this project's headless
pipeline with the installed Piper binary and `en_US-lessac-medium` model,
at speed 1.0. Mono PCM16, 22050 Hz. No microphone recording was used.

Transcript:

> Hello! I am Parrot. Highlight some text, press your shortcut, and I will read
> it aloud. Pause whenever you like, or change the speaking speed. Your words
> stay on your computer.

Reproduce from the repository root after installing the voice:

```sh
cargo run --locked -- --engine piper --text 'Hello! I am Parrot. Highlight some text, press your shortcut, and I will read it aloud. Pause whenever you like, or change the speaking speed. Your words stay on your computer.' --wav docs/voice-demo.wav
```

[Upstream model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/lessac/medium/MODEL_CARD)
identifies the Lessac Blizzard 2013 dataset and links its license. Model weights
and the Piper executable are not bundled in this repository.
